A quantity column is not enough. Check whether inventory tracking is on for those variants, and whether the product CSV is even the right file for your store's location setup.

You imported products with an Inventory quantity column and no errors, yet the variants show 0 stock. Before re-importing with bigger numbers, check two separate things: the tracking setting and the file type.

Check tracking before quantity

  1. Check one affected variant in the admin. Open the product, then the variant, and look at the Inventory section. Shopify's setup guide says you must activate inventory tracking for a product before you can view or adjust its inventory levels. If tracking is off for that variant, a quantity in a CSV has nowhere to go.
  2. Check the tracking column in your file. In Shopify's current product CSV format, the Inventory tracker column controls which service tracks the variant's inventory. shopify means Shopify tracks it; blank means inventory is not tracked. Shopify's documentation also notes that removing existing tracking options stops tracking.
  3. Keep dependent columns consistent. Shopify warns that when Inventory tracker is set to shopify, the Continue selling when out of stock and Fulfillment service columns can't be left blank, and partial related-column values can cause an import error. Decide your out-of-stock policy deliberately (deny or continue) instead of accepting defaults by accident. Note Shopify's caution: Continue selling when out of stock doesn't apply to Shopify POS.
  4. Check whether the product CSV can carry quantities at all. Shopify says the product CSV's Inventory quantity column applies only to stores with a single location, and defaults to 0 when empty. If you have more than one location, use the separate inventory CSV export/import instead. The product CSV is for product data; the inventory CSV is for quantities per location.
  5. If you use the inventory CSV, review before you import. Export All states, edit a copy, and compare it with a fresh export before importing. The free Vertex Inventory change review reads both files in your browser and shows planned quantity changes, duplicate identities and stale snapshot values. It never connects to your store or imports anything. Keep Shopify's own On hand (current) safety validation in the file you import.
Fictional example: A merchant creates 40 variants by CSV with Inventory quantity set to 25 on every row, but the Inventory tracker column is empty because the supplier's template didn't include it. After import, every variant shows 0. Nothing was "lost"; tracking was never on, so there was no quantity to record. Turning tracking on and setting quantities deliberately fixes the cause, not the symptom. Fictional illustration, not a customer result.

Do not compensate blindly. Do not respond to a 0-stock import by inflating quantities, switching Continue selling to continue, or clearing On hand (current) to skip safety validation. Each of those changes store behavior. Fix the tracking setting and file type first.

Tool limit: Vertex's reviewer works on Shopify All states inventory CSVs. It does not check your product CSV's tracking columns, turn tracking on, or see your store's location count. Its checks are a second review, not import approval.
