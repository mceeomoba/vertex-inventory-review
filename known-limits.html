Known limits of the free review tools.

Four free browser tools help you review Shopify and Ecwid CSV files before import. This page lists what they do not check, cannot see and will not promise, so you can decide how much to rely on them.

Written from the live tools on October 20, 2026.

All four tools

  • They are review aids, not import approval. Shopify's and Ecwid's own previews and import rules still decide what imports.
  • Your files are read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account or store connection. The hosting provider can still log ordinary page visits.
  • The tools cannot see your live store or anything changed after your export.
  • Downloaded reports stay wherever you save them. Keep them private, and never put private store files in public issues.
  • They are free experimental releases. No support commitment or response time is promised.

Inventory change review (Shopify)

  • Shopify All states inventory CSVs only (UTF-8, comma-separated). A product catalog export or an Available-only export stops the review with a message naming the file; export Products > Inventory > Export > All states instead.
  • Duplicate identities or missing variant fields stop the review. Rows are never guessed or merged.
  • A new location, or a baseline row marked not stocked, needs manual review.
  • It cannot see your permissions, inventory policies or Shopify's final validation. A clean review is not a guarantee of import success.
  • Locations are case sensitive. SKU alone never matches rows.

Product CSV preflight (Shopify)

  • It checks a limited set of fields: not images, linked metafields, market pricing, tax or shipping rules, actual store records, or every Shopify field.
  • A REVIEW note is a prompt to look, not proof of an error. Reused SKUs or repeated variants can be intentional.
  • It never changes, fixes or deletes a row.
  • A clean report does not prove a valid or safe import. Blank columns can still overwrite existing values, and changed option values can replace variant IDs.
  • Findings reference parsed CSV record numbers, which can differ from physical line numbers in files with multiline fields. Files up to 15 MB.

Snapshot comparison (Shopify)

  • It compares two exports you already have. No live monitoring, alerts or background checks.
  • Added or missing refers to export membership only. A missing row can mean a different export scope, not a deletion.
  • A quantity change does not prove a sale, a loss or an inventory error.
  • It needs All states inventory exports and reads On hand (current); On hand (new) edit columns are ignored.
  • The screen shows up to 200 matching rows; the downloaded CSV contains every compared row. Files up to 15 MB.

Ecwid Import Scope Check

  • It checks omitted options, input fields and variations only: not prices, stock, images, every import rule, or your live store.
  • It needs a fresh, complete catalog export from the same store. An incomplete baseline can hide omissions.
  • A changed option name or type can appear as an omission even when intentional. New products, changed parent SKUs or IDs, translated headers and other ambiguous mappings need manual review.
  • Products missing from the import are not marked as deleted.
  • The report is not a corrected import file. Files up to 10 MB.
